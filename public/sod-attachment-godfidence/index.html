Style 1

Anxious.

Driven by a deep craving for connection. Acts fast to soothe the feeling.

What it looks like

  • Needing constant reassurance
  • Over-explaining, over-texting
  • Emotionally intense or reactive
  • Investigating, overthinking
  • Escalating when feeling insecure

Avoidant.

Driven by a deep need for independence and self-protection. Thinks first, feels later.

What it looks like

  • Pulling away when things get serious
  • Avoiding hard conversations
  • Struggling to express needs
  • Keeping options open
  • Feeling overwhelmed by closeness

Primary coping

ESCAPE

Uses BLAME to justify the distance: "they're too needy."

Secure.

Not about never getting triggered — about what you DO when you are. Feel, think, then act.

The secure shift.

  • Instead of blame→They take ownership
  • Instead of shame→They choose honesty and vulnerability
  • Instead of control→They practice trust and flexibility
  • Instead of escape→They choose engagement

Same emotions. Different choice.

You can be more than one attachment style — but you often have a primary.

Different people. Different situations. Different parts of your story. The goal isn't a label — it's awareness.

Part Two

Godfidence.

The truth God says about you — claimed daily.

Definition

Confidence in the God-image of your being.

The belief that the unique traits and abilities in you came from God — and you carry them into every relationship.

Your turn

The Godfidence List.

Write 10 qualities God created in YOU that you bring into every relationship.

Get to 2, 4, 6 — beginning IS the win. We'll keep building it.

"I am fearfully and wonderfully made."

— Psalm 139:14

More verses in your workbook: Ephesians 2:10 · Matthew 6:26 · Lamentations 3:22-23 · Isaiah 54:10

God loves you uniquely — not equally.

— Lisa Bevere · Without Rival

There is something only YOU bring. Your Godfidence list names it.

Examples to get you started.

  • I am compassionate
  • I am loyal
  • I see people deeply
  • I bring joy to a room
  • I love hard
  • I'm honest
  • I'm steady
  • I make people feel safe
  • I'm a great listener
  • I bring courage to my circle

Yours will be different. That's the point.

The daily practice

Read it before every date.

Before the first message. Before the meeting. Before the spiral. Your Godfidence list goes with you.

Homework

Build your list.

Ask 3 friends: "What do I bring to your life?"

Add their answers to your list.

Pray over it.

Read it every day before Phase 2.
