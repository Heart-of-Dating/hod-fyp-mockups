The Peace
Cycle.

Last time we named the pain. Tonight we learn to answer it.

The question

How do you move from
pain to peace?

Even if your circumstances don't change, you can. You are actually instructed by God to emotionally self-regulate.

You are not powerless.

God calls you to self-control. Key word: self. Your regulation does not depend on other people.

What Scripture says

"A man without self-control is like a city broken into and left without walls."

Proverbs 25:28

"Whoever is slow to anger is better than the mighty, and he who rules his spirit than he who takes a city."

Proverbs 16:32

"God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control."

2 Timothy 1:7
A fair warning

Self-control does not come naturally.

  • It takes a lot of effort.
  • It is not popular.
  • And it is the doorway to everything else.
Remember the Pain Cycle

Every cope is protecting a belief.

When you blame, shame, control, or escape, your brain believes you are unloved or unsafe — and copes to protect you.

The Peace Cycle asks one question: is that pain message true?

The path

Four steps.

1

Name the feeling

Say what you feel. Out loud. Your words have power.

2

Identify your default

Name what you're normally tempted to do in the moment.

3

Replace with truth

Instead, choose what you are going to believe.

4

Choose a new action

Because of that truth, here is what you'll do differently.

2

Name your default.

  • Say what you normally do.
  • In the moment, what are you tempted to reach for?
  • Blame, shame, control, or escape?
3

Replace with truth.

Is our pain message true?

How do we know? That's the burning question.

Where we get the truth about our pain messages

Three sources.

1

Other people

What do the people who love you actually say about these messages?

2

God & Scripture

What does God say about your pain message? As a Christian, this is our most authoritative truth.

3

Yourself

The most powerful of all. As an adult, you can give yourself a new message about who you are.

You have the choice to decide what you let stand.

As an adult, you can learn to give yourself a new message about who you are.

Here is the whole thing

The test isn't do we believe the truth. It's whether we believe it in the moment of pain.

When you connect to truth in a moment of pain, the truth sets you free by speaking directly to the pain in the moment.

In-class exercise

From pain to peace — your turn.

Go to the pain moment. Imagine God, and a close friend you love.

Is it true that you are (your pain message)?

What do these people say about that?

4

Choose a new action.

Because you believe a new truth, you can show up as a new person. Here is what that looks like.

Instead of the fight

Blame
becomes
Nurture.

  • Love
  • Encourage
  • Accept
  • Support
  • Be kind
  • Listen
  • Be patient
  • Be compassionate
Instead of the flight

Shame
becomes
Self-Value.

  • Value self
  • Respect self
  • Remain positive
  • Stay humble
  • Offer optimism
  • Be hopeful
  • Be self-aware
  • Display confidence
Instead of the fight

Control
becomes
Balance.

Every little thing is gonna be alright.

  • Be vulnerable
  • Open
  • Appreciate
  • Imperfect
  • Flexible
  • Relaxed
  • Spontaneous
  • Let things go
Instead of the flight

Escape
becomes
Connect.

  • Responsible
  • Reliable
  • Self-controlled
  • Connected
  • Intimate
  • Faithful
  • Forthcoming
  • A planner
  • Honest — no ghosting

The new you in Jesus

Look at these actions. This could be you.

Jesus wants this for you. So does the body of Christ, your people, your family. Because you believe a new truth.

Put it together

Say it in four lines.

1 · Say what you feel — name it out loud.
2 · Say what you normally do in the moment.
3 · Say the truth — "Instead, I am choosing to believe…"
4 · Say what you'll do differently — "Because of this truth…"

The Peace Cycle does not remove the pain. It changes your response to it.

You can choose truth in the very moment of pain. That is the whole freedom.

Homework

Practice the peace.

Identify your default coping mechanism, and write your common pain message.

Replace it with truth.

Then practice a new response the next time the moment comes.
